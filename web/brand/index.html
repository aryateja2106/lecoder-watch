The mark

One glyph, two grounds.

The source file (Lesearch Logo.svg) hardcodes its color: a pure-black ring at #000000, and every arc and node at #231F20 — a near-black gray, clearly tuned against a white page. The path data underneath is fine; the colors are baked in. Below, every stroke and fill is swapped for currentColor instead, so one color declaration drives the whole mark on either ground.

Usage rules

Minimums, clearspace, and what never to do.

Minimum size

The nine-node mesh is the whole idea, but it's also the first thing to break. Below ~24px it compresses into visual noise — look back at the 16–18px renders above. The ring alone stays legible to about 12px. Never scale the full mark below 24px; at 16–18px, the favicon and menu-bar crops shown above are already as small as this mark should go.

Clearspace

Minimum clearspace on every side is 20% of the mark's own height — nothing else (text, edges, other marks) crosses the dashed line below.

Dashed line = minimum clearspace, drawn to scale.

Never

✕Stretch or skew non-uniformly

✕Rotate the mark

✕Recolor the ring and mesh separately

✕Add drop shadows or glow effects

✕Place on low-contrast grounds

✕Crop the ring
