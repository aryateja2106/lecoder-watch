Same deck, three latchable keys + Dictate inject on the wrist.
1Sticky latch is the headline. ctrl & ⌘ held active with an accent border, fill, and a small pin — so a queued ⌃⌘-chord is legible before you inject. Single-fire keys (shift, esc, del) stay monochrome.
2The strip never lies about its contents. esc / del survive to the end; win + opt are demoted into the ⋯ overflow rather than clipped, fixing the cut-off last key in the original.
3Deck floats, never a navbar. Translucent over the full-bleed canvas, it collapses to a labeled handle (“Deck · 2 latched”) via the chevron — reclaiming the framebuffer the OS keyboard used to eat.
4Status colors earn their place. Green link LED + 42 ms latency and the “Live” jump-to-latest chip are the only color besides the one blue accent; the terminal keeps real ANSI green/amber inside its frame.
5Eye = preview/hi-res toggle (latched blue here), and the injection pill carries a blinking accent caret to read as a live text target, not a button.
